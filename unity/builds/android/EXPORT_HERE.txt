Export your Unity project as an Android library to this folder.

Expected structure after Unity export:
- unity/builds/android/unityLibrary/
- unity/builds/android/launcher/
- unity/builds/android/build.gradle

After export, remove the <intent-filter>...</intent-filter> from:
- unity/builds/android/unityLibrary/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml

Then run from RN root:
- npx expo run:android
